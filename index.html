How it works

Drop once. Fetch anywhere.

DropNest never pushes anything at you. Each device keeps its own box; the others come and take what they need.

Drop

On your phone, use the Share button in WhatsApp, Instagram, Gallery or your browser. On the PC, drag files onto the window or press Paste. Items stay in your box until you delete them.

Meet

Open DropNest on both devices on the same Wi-Fi — or turn on hotspot mode on your phone when there is no router. They find each other in seconds.

Fetch

Open the other device's box, tick what you want and press Fetch. Files land in your Gallery or Downloads; links and text go straight to your clipboard.

Questions

Good to know

Does DropNest need the internet?

No. Devices talk to each other directly over your Wi-Fi. If there is no router — on a train, in a car — turn on hotspot mode on the phone and connect the PC to it.

Phone to phone, or PC to PC?

Yes. Any two devices running DropNest can open each other's box: two Androids, two Windows PCs, or one of each.

Where do fetched files go?

On Android into Pictures, Movies, Music or Download → DropNest (no storage permission needed). On Windows into Downloads\DropNest, or any folder you choose in Settings.

Can someone on my Wi-Fi read my box?

Only if you let them. The default asks you every time a device wants in; you can restrict it to trusted devices or add a PIN. Transfers are encrypted with a per-device certificate.

iPhone or Mac?

The Windows build also compiles for macOS and Linux from the open-source repository. An iPhone version is not available yet.

Is it free?

Yes — free on both stores, no ads, no in-app purchases.
