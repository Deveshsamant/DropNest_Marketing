Privacy policy

DropNest keeps your data on your devices.

Effective 18 September 2026. This policy covers the DropNest apps for Android and Windows and this website.

The short version

  • No accounts. You never sign up or sign in.
  • No servers. DropNest has no cloud. Files, text and links move directly between your own devices on your local network.
  • No analytics, no ads, no trackers. The apps contain no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and never phone home.
  • No data is collected by us. We do not receive, store or process anything you drop into your box.

Everything below stays on the device it was created on. You can delete it at any time from inside the app or by uninstalling it.

What other devices can see

On your local network DropNest announces your device name, its local IP address and certificate fingerprint so that other DropNest devices can find it. Another device can only list or fetch the contents of your box after you allow it (once, always, or by PIN), unless you deliberately set your box to “Anyone nearby”. These announcements never leave your Wi-Fi or hotspot network.

Permissions

Android asks for permissions that DropNest uses only for the features described:

  • Network / Wi-Fi state, multicast — discovering devices on the local network and transferring between them.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices (Android 13+) or location (Android 12 and older) — required by Android to create a local-only hotspot. DropNest does not read or store your location.
  • Notifications and foreground service — keeps receiving in the background and shows transfer progress. Started only when you open the app; stop it from the notification.
  • Photos, videos, files — only the items you explicitly share or pick are copied into your box. DropNest does not scan your library.

On Windows, the first launch triggers the Windows Firewall prompt because DropNest listens on your private network (TCP 47843, UDP 47842). It does not require administrator rights.

Security

Every transfer is encrypted with TLS using a certificate generated on your device. Devices verify each other's certificate fingerprint, so a device pretending to be one you trust is rejected. Your box can additionally require a PIN. DropNest cannot decrypt your transfers — there is no intermediary.

This website

This site is static and sets no cookies. It remembers your light/dark choice in your browser only. It is hosted on Vercel, whose servers may log standard request data (IP address, user agent) for operating the service; see Vercel's privacy policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Children

DropNest is a general-purpose utility not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with an updated effective date. Since the apps collect nothing, changes will typically only describe new local features.
